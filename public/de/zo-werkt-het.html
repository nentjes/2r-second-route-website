How 2R works

2R is your travelling companion that tells stories. You start it, you drive, and along the way you hear stories about the places you pass. Here is everything you need to know — in a few minutes.

🚗 Getting started

In the app, tap Start the journey and allow location access. 2R automatically narrates the places you pass — no account, nothing to set up first.

🎵 Alongside your music

Play your own music (Spotify, Apple Music, the radio — anything). It automatically fades down when a story starts and swells back up when it ends. Turn on “Music tips per region” for a tap toward fitting music along the way. 2R never streams music itself.

🔵 The blue bar while driving

If you switch to Maps or your music app, iOS shows a blue bar at the top indicating 2R is using your location. This is expected — it lets 2R keep narrating in the background. Tap it to return to 2R.

🔊 Choosing your voice

Under Settings → Voice, pick your narrating voice and the tone: natural (calm and economical) or expressive (the most human, with hesitations and sighs). “Preview this voice” lets you hear it right away.

🧭 How you travel

Tell 2R whether you go by car, bike, on foot or by train. It adapts the pace and distance of the stories accordingly.

🎯 Travel quiz for the family

Turn on the travel quiz for one question after each story. The passenger taps the answer — with a scoreboard per trip.

🧪 Try it standing still

Want to try 2R without driving? Use the Route Simulator in the menu to simulate a trip.

Ready to drive?

2R runs live in TestFlight and on the web.
