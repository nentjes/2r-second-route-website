Across Europe

The source is local.
The story travels with you.

From a Norwegian fjord to the olive groves of Puglia, 2R looks for the stories that belong to a place — preferably in local sources — and retells them in your language.

North · Sognefjord, Norway
Landscape · geology

The morning begins where the mountains meet the water.

Drawing on Norwegian sources, 2R reveals how ice shaped this landscape thousands of years before a road or railway appeared.

West · Burgundy, France
Heritage · village life

An abbey appears between the plane trees.

Local heritage sources connect the old stones to the village still living around them. Not a postcard, but a place with a memory.

East · South Bohemia, Czechia
Architecture · history

The tower turns the horizon into a chapter.

Czech sources show how architectural styles and ideas travelled from Vienna to this small hilltop town.

South · Puglia, Italy
People · landscape

The evening light brings everyone to a brief stillness.

Local stories of olive growing reveal how people, stone and trees have lived together here for centuries, with heat and drought.

Why 2R is different

Not an encyclopedia. A travel companion.

Existing travel-narrator apps rely on pre-recorded scripts — limited to popular routes, the same story for everyone. 2R generates live, everywhere, and adapts to your interests.

I

Local sources first

Municipal sites, tourism boards and heritage organisations — not just Wikipedia. Abroad, local-language sources are used and retold in your own language.

II

Guide mode

One continuous story with the region as its through-line — country, province, area, town — instead of disconnected facts.

III

Always honest

Route never pretends to know what you can see. Every story says whether it was written on the spot or reviewed by an editor, with a button to report an error.

IV

Privacy first

No account or password. Your location is not stored by default — only if you enable it. You can always view, export or erase what 2R has kept.

V

Solo or family

Choose your interests, listen together and play a travel quiz along the way. You decide how much space the narrator gets.

VI

Works anywhere

Tested in the Netherlands and on a drive through Lombardy, Italy — same quality, narrated in your own language.
