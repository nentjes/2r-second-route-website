🗺️ 2R · Second Route

One route gets you there.
The second one tells you what you're seeing.

2R is an AI travel guide that rides along with your navigation. While your first route (Google Maps, Waze) gets you from A to B, Route — the voice of 2R — narrates live what you're passing: the history, the nature, the local stories. Tuned to your own interests.

How it works

From driving to listening, in three steps

1

Turn Route on

One switch. Pick your interests — history, nature, football, photography — or type in your own.

2

Drive, cycle or walk

2R follows your route via GPS and searches live for what's worth telling — from local sources, not just Wikipedia.

3

Listen to Route

A warm, expressive voice narrates — honestly about what you can actually see, never pretending.

Why 2R is different

Not an encyclopedia. A travel companion.

Existing travel-narrator apps rely on pre-recorded scripts — limited to popular routes, the same story for everyone. 2R generates live, everywhere, and adapts to your interests.

📍

Local sources first

Municipal sites, tourism boards and heritage organisations — not just Wikipedia. Abroad, local-language sources are used and retold in your own language.

🎙️

Guide mode

One continuous story with the region as its through-line — country, province, area, town — instead of disconnected facts.

🤥

Always honest

Route never pretends to know what you can see. Close by: "look now." Further away: "worth a detour" — never the other way around.

🔒

Privacy-first

No location storage, no account required. Every request stands on its own.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Solo or family

Your own interests, or content for the whole car — with games and quizzes for the road in the works.

🌍

Works anywhere

Tested in the Netherlands and on a drive through Lombardy, Italy — same quality, narrated in your own language.
