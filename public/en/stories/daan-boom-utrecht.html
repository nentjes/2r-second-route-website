Daan Boom, Utrecht media talent

Utrecht, Nederland · 17 augustus 2026 · narrated by Route

Utrecht is of course known for its fishing heritage and traditional dress, but it's just as much the ultimate birthplace of Dutch trendsetters and public darlings. Take Daan Boom, born and raised in the city, who started his career as a child star and grew into a true media icon with the show Streetlab. Long before social media existed, people here already knew how to win over an audience.
