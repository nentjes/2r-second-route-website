Villa Horev

Bilthoven, Nederland · 15 augustus 2026 · narrated by Route

Look at Villa Horev, here on the corner of Soestdijkseweg Zuid and Boslaan. The name is a beautiful acronym for "Hope for Rest and Peace." That rest is what the first resident, Mr Reijsenbach, was seeking after his years as president of the Javasche Bank in what was then the Dutch East Indies. Around 1900, many wealthy returnees moved to the healthy woodlands of Bilthoven.
