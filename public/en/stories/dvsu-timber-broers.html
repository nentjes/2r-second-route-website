Football

Football club DVSU and the Timber brothers

De Bilt, Nederland · 17 augustus 2026 · narrated by Route

On your left, a few hundred metres away, lies a piece of pure football history: the amateur club DVSU. The name stands for "United Through Friendship, Utrecht." It all began here in De Bilt and the surrounding area back in 1932. Here's the thing: even the well-known football twins Jurriën and Quinten Timber once took their first steps on this pitch! From muddy fields to a breeding ground for internationals.
