Privacy

Your journey stays yours.

What 2R uses, and for how long.

Last updated: 23-10-2026

This information applies to app build 124. Voice questions and partner tips are not yet available; these arrangements apply when those features are introduced.

What 2R uses, and for how long.

2R uses your location to tell you about what you pass. Your position is sent to our server for that purpose and kept there for at most seven days, to improve your own experience and to fix mistakes. After that it is deleted automatically.

If you ask 2R a question with your voice, the recording is sent to our server to turn it into text. We do not keep the recording; the text of your question is kept for at most seven days.

We do not build a profile of you, we do not sell anything on, and we do not share your data with others. The companies that produce our stories and voices (Google, OpenAI) and our server (Cloudflare) work on our behalf.

Tips from partners along the way are off unless you switch them on in the settings.

There is no account, no password and no e-mail address. Under the gear icon, at "What 2R keeps about you", you can see what is known from your device, export it and delete everything. Testers are fully logged; that is stated when you join.

Other data and your choices

Technical logs and generated stories are kept for at most 90 and 90 days respectively; reports of incorrect stories for at most 365 days. Purchase records are subject to the statutory retention period and are not erased when you delete your log. “Keep nothing” stops logging. This does not apply to testers: their activities are fully logged, as explained in advance.
