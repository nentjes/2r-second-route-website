🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy — 2R

Last updated: August 2026

2R (working title: MapsInfo) is a travel-guide app that narrates the world around you while you drive, cycle or walk. This policy explains exactly what data the app uses and why — no fine print.

Location

  • Your GPS location is used to determine which stories are relevant.
  • Your location is never stored on our servers and never logged. Every request to the server contains only the name and context of a specific place — no location history, no trip logging.

Stories & voice (processors)

  • To generate stories we send place names and brief context to Google (Gemini API).
  • To narrate the text aloud, that text is sent to ElevenLabs for voice synthesis.
  • Factual information is fetched directly from your device from Wikipedia, and route data from OpenStreetMap / OSRM — these requests never pass through our server.
  • Generated stories and audio fragments are cached temporarily (reused for other users at the same location) to reduce cost and wait time.

Usage limits

To prevent abuse we keep a technical, temporary counter per IP address (kept for a maximum of 48 hours). This is only a number, never linked to a profile, name or account, and never shared with third parties.

Accounts

The app requires no account, login or registration.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nimco@nentjes.nl.
