Our quality compass

Quality is not a promise.
It is a visible process.

2R wants to inspire wonder without suggesting more certainty than the sources allow. That is why origin, editing and corrections remain visible — including while the product is still developing.

From source to voice

Five moments when a story has to improve.

01

Find

We prefer local, public and authoritative sources.

02

Compare

Facts are checked against each other; doubt is not polished away.

03

Remove

Anything that cannot be supported well enough does not enter the story.

04

Edit

Route books receive human final editing for content, rhythm and tone.

05

Improve

Sources stay visible and users can report an error.

Two kinds of story

The same honesty, a different making process.

01

Live on the road

The app searches around your current location and creates a story on the spot. It is personal and current; sources and error reporting therefore remain visible.

02

Editorial route book

A fixed route receives advance source research, connected chapters and human final editing. That label appears with the route.

Never “finished”

Reporting an error is part of quality.

No chain of sources is infallible. 2R therefore promises no perfect truth, but a verifiable method: show the source, receive a report, correct it and explain material changes where needed.
