The method

From source material to a living route.

01

The route

You supply a GPX file, route description or existing guide. We map the actual trail and its meaningful surroundings.

02

The knowledge

Local sources, heritage archives and steward information become the raw material. Every fact remains traceable.

03

The route book

Chapters gain one narrative thread, the right walking or cycling pace and a final human edit.

04

The experience

Publish in 2R, on the web or through a QR code — with map, audio, reading text and source notes.

A GPX file shows the way. 2R tells the story of that way.

The next horizon

From pilot to 2R Studio.

We deliberately begin with a few routes made together with their stewards. That practice can grow into a Studio where organisations manage routes, sources and chapters themselves, while 2R safeguards quality, voice and distribution.

Which route deserves a voice first?

Send us a GPX file, guide or route page. Together we will choose one route to prove the content, technology and audience response.
