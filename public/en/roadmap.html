Roadmap

Where 2R came from, and where we're going

2R started as an idea during a road trip: there was so much to see along the way, and no one to explain it. Here's what's been built since, and what's next.

Live today
✓ Live today

AI stories from local sources

Generated live per location, prioritising municipal sites, tourism boards and heritage organisations — not just Wikipedia.

✓ Live today

Expressive narration voice

A warm, natural voice — with sighs, laughter and natural speech, instead of a robotic one.

✓ Live today

Guide mode

One continuous story with the region as its through-line — country, province, area, town — instead of disconnected snippets per location.

✓ Live today

Works across Europe

Abroad, the local Wikipedia is used (Italian, French, German…) and retold in your own language.

✓ Live today

Nine interests, and your own

History, nature, art, sport, food & drink and more — plus a free-text field for any other interest.

In TestFlight
In TestFlight

The iOS app

2R runs as a real iPhone app in closed testing, with its own 2R icon and background location.

In TestFlight

Background audio, with your own music underneath

Route keeps narrating with the screen off or your navigation app in front, and your own music (Spotify, radio, podcast) automatically ducks the moment the narrator starts — and swells back when the story ends.

Coming soon
Coming soon

Traveler stories

People who know their own region can add their own stories — typed or recorded — with attribution and human approval before publishing.

Coming soon

Road music from 2R itself

Beyond your own music, 2R will also be able to play fitting road music of its own between the stories. Which genre is up to you — road music is personal.

Coming soon

Hands-free voice feedback

"That's not right", "tell me more" or "five minutes of quiet" — all by voice, because the driver never touches the phone.

Coming soon

Family mode with games

Content for the whole car, with quizzes and games for the road.
